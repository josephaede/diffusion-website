Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

In short: Diffusion runs entirely on your Mac. The app has no server. It exchanges data only with the platforms you connect (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta). It uses no advertising, analytics or audience measurement, and it does not sell or share your data.

1. Who we are

Diffusion is a personal macOS app published by Joseph (jojobabyfoot@gmail.com). In this policy, "the app" means Diffusion and "you" means the person using it on their Mac.

2. How the app works

Diffusion runs locally on macOS. To connect a network, it opens the platform's official authorization page in your browser. The platform then sends an authorization code back to a small temporary local server, reachable only on your Mac at http://localhost:8765/callback. This server closes as soon as the code is received, when the connection is cancelled, or after 5 minutes.

The publisher operates no server and receives no data. All requests go directly from your Mac to the platforms' official APIs.

3. Data processed

YouTube (Google)

Requested scopes: youtube.upload, youtube and youtube.readonly. They are used to:

  • upload your videos, their thumbnail and their metadata (title, description, tags, category, privacy status, publication date, playlist);
  • read your channel name, subscriber count, total view count and the list of your playlists;
  • read your 25 most recent videos: title, date, thumbnail, view, like and comment counts.

TikTok

Requested scopes: user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list, video.publish and video.upload. They are used to:

  • read your display name, username, avatar and profile counters (followers, following, likes, video count);
  • read your account's posting options (available privacy levels, maximum duration);
  • upload your videos with their text and the options you chose, either as a direct post or to your TikTok inbox;
  • read your 20 most recent videos: title, description, date, cover, link, views, likes, comments and shares.

Instagram and Facebook (Meta)

Requested permissions: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_manage_posts and, optionally, read_insights and instagram_manage_insights. They are used to:

  • list the Facebook Pages you manage and the Instagram professional account linked to each one;
  • check which permissions you actually granted;
  • publish your videos as Reels on Instagram and on the chosen Facebook Page, with their text and options;
  • read the Page name, the Instagram username and the follower count;
  • read your 25 most recent posts on each network: text, date, link, thumbnail, likes, comments and, if the permission is granted, views and shares.

Content you publish

The videos, thumbnails and text you choose are read on your Mac, then sent only to the platforms you select for publication.

4. Storage on your Mac

  • Tokens and credentials: access and refresh tokens, as well as the developer app credentials you enter (IDs and secret keys), are stored in your macOS session's Keychain. They are never written to the data file below.
  • App data: settings, publication history, the latest statistics retrieved, daily follower history, the current draft and preferences. They are stored in the file ~/Library/Application Support/Diffusion/donnees.json. The app does not encrypt this file: it is protected by your macOS session's permissions.
  • Thumbnails shown in the statistics are loaded from the platforms' servers when displayed.
  • Notifications (upload finished, error) are local macOS notifications.

5. Data sharing

Your data is sent only to the platforms you connect, and only for the purposes described above. Those platforms process it under their own privacy policies: Google, TikTok, Meta.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, audience measurement or third-party crash reporting. The publisher does not sell, rent or share any data.

6. Google API data

Diffusion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is used only for the features described in this policy. It is not transferred to anyone and is not used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting a network in Diffusion (Accounts › Disconnect) deletes its tokens from the Keychain and the statistics stored for that network.
  • Publication history and follower history are kept until you delete the donnees.json file. Deleting this file and the ~/Library/Application Support/Diffusion folder erases all app data.
  • Developer app credentials remain in the Keychain. You can delete them with the Keychain Access app by searching for items of the fr.perso.diffusion service.
  • On the platforms: disconnecting in Diffusion does not revoke access on the platform side. To revoke it:
    • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions;
    • Facebook: Settings › Business integrations;
    • TikTok: the app's security settings, under authorized apps.
  • Published videos remain on the platforms. You can delete them from each platform.

8. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no tracking scripts. GitHub may log technical connection data, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

9. Your rights

App data stays on your Mac: you can view, change or delete it directly (see section 7). For any question about this policy, write to jojobabyfoot@gmail.com.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated if the way the app works changes. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.

Diffusion

A personal macOS app to publish short videos to several networks at once and track their statistics.

Publish everywhere

One video, with its title, caption, hashtags and thumbnail, sent to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Schedule

Pick a publication date and see upcoming posts in a calendar.

Track statistics

Followers, views, likes and comments, per network and overall, based on the data each platform provides.

How it works

Diffusion runs only on its user's Mac. It connects to each network through the platform's official authorization flow (OAuth), then talks directly to its official APIs:

  • YouTube Data API (Google);
  • TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API;
  • Meta Graph API, for Instagram and Facebook Pages.

Diffusion uses no intermediate server: videos, text and statistics go directly between the Mac and the platform concerned. Details are in the privacy policy.
